What is it?

This web application prototype to make building smoothies for an oder be more fun and efficient.

Description: Users can pick between different cups and fruits to build their perfect smoothie.

The Design

The design uses green accents to convey a sense of sustainability and freshness. It is also inspired by selection games like dress up games in which you can swipe and choose the items you like.
